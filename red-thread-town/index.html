🧶 Red Thread Town

Six people in this town wear a letter: Ava the doctor, Ben the office worker, Cole the cop, Daisy the rich kid, Eddie the gym addict and Fiona the foodie. Everyone else just lives here. You are the matchmaker.

  1. Turn on the camera and raise a hand. The view pulls back to a bird's-eye view.
  2. Pinch one person with your thumb and index finger, then pinch another. A red thread ties them together.
  3. Lower your hand. The director cam shows you their calls, dates, confessions and fights.
  4. If they don't get along, they break up. The thread snaps and you can pair them again.

Hand tracking runs on your device; the camera image is never uploaded. All people and stories are fictional.
